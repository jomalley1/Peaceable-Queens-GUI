Peaceable Queens Companion

Two equal armies of queens on an n×n board, with no queen attacking anyone on the other side. This is a separate companion board: import and export layouts, check them against OEIS A250000, and load verified record attempts. The original GUI is unchanged.

Your original board OEIS A250000 MathWorld

Board

Choose a size up to 50×50. In auto-black mode, click to place or remove white queens; every unattacked square counts as black. In manual mode, place both armies yourself.

Import and export

Paste a text grid of W, B, and . characters (Q, w, and b are also accepted, and spaces are ignored), or a compact list such as n=8 plus lines like W 1 2 or W: (1,2) (1,3) or algebraic W: a8, b8.


          

        

      

    


    

      
Known best values

      
Exact values a(1) through a(20) are from OEIS A250000, including the November 2026 proofs for n = 16 to 20. For n = 21 to 50 the figures are the best-known lower bounds from Ainley’s construction, ⌊7n²/48⌋ (n = 27 was first listed as 105 and later improved to 106).

Record attempts

      
Verified layouts from the current search, one per board size. Each was checked so that no white queen and black queen share a row, column, or diagonal. Load any of them onto the board.
